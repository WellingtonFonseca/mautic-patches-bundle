# Patches: Status column of the contact list, the same badges on the contact page, and the badges of the email list

The Status column of the contact list (one badge per channel: Email, SMS, HSM, green or red by Do Not Contact), a copy of those three badges left of the Editar button of the contact page, and the badges of the email list (type, A/B test, translation, draft) beside the status. Both are made by Resources/js/list-status-toggle.js from what core already prints, with no query added. The look and the layout of a Status column are in wiki/patches-status-column.md.

## Traps

- The contact page script loads only when the `page.header.left` event carries the contact as `lead` (ContactDetailStatusSubscriber). Other pages that fire the event skip it.
- StatusScript prints the list script on every call and each run replaces `mauticPatchesListStatusApi`: the install call must stay right after its own companion tag.
- The channel of a contact page badge is the last word of core's Do Not Contact label. A language that puts the channel in the middle of the sentence would break it: check the translation of `mautic.lead.do.not.contact_channel` first.
- list-status-toggle.js is near 600 lines: new list or page logic goes in its own file, like contact-detail-badges.js.
- The column is added only once the page is parsed (`document.readyState` not 'loading', and again at DOMContentLoaded): a half-drawn table got its column once, with rows missing. Specs that read the headers of the contact list wait for `th.col-contact-status` (segment-page.spec.js does).
- segment-page.spec.js compares the contact list headers with the segment's contacts tab: it drops 'Status', which only the contact list has.
- The cells of `#leadTable` are nowrap like the other lists (wiki/patches-status-column.md, `badges box and layout`).
