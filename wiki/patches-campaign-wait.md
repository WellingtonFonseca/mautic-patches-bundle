# Patches: campaign action Wait and Recalculate dates

The campaign action Wait (`patches.wait_relative`): a date per contact counted from a date field of the contact or of a Custom Object item, the scheduler subclass, Recalculate dates with simulation, the command and the box of the Ações tab.

## Campaign action: Wait (patches.wait_relative) and Recalculate dates

An ACTION (pt_BR "Esperar", en_US "Wait") that holds the contact until a date counted from a date field of the contact or of a Custom Object item ("12 days after disciplina.inicio"), the same date for everyone with the same date, whenever they joined the campaign. It sends nothing; the events after it run when its date comes. Core's waits count from the moment the contact reaches the event, so the student who joined 4 days late left 4 days late.
