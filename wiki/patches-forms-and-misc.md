# Patches: date and time fields, translation overrides, email builder modal, plugin icon, left menu

The date and time fields formatted while typing, the translation overrides of the pt_BR pack, the add button of the lists of values, the email builder validation error modal, the plugin icon and the left menu that opens on hover.

## Left menu: a strip that opens on hover

```json
{
  "what": "The left menu of the admin is a 15px strip with a play arrow (remixicon ri-play-fill, \\f00a). The mouse over the strip shows the whole menu (230px) ON TOP of the page; the page does not move. The mouse away hides it again. The keyboard focus inside the menu opens it too (:has(:focus-visible)). Not :focus-within: a clicked link keeps the focus (ajax navigation), so the menu would stay open after the mouse left. A touch screen of 768px or more has no hover: the menu opens on a tap on the strip and closes on a tap elsewhere (browser sticky hover); not tested.",
  "code": "EventListener/SidebarRevealOnHoverSubscriber.php: pure CSS in the <head> (CoreEvents::VIEW_INJECT_CUSTOM_ASSETS, addStyleDeclaration). transform:translateX(-215px) on .app-sidebar.sidebar-left, the strip is its ::after, and #app-content and #app-footer get margin-left 15px.",
  "breakpoint": "Only from 768px up (core's screen-sm). Below it core hides the menu behind its own toggle; the rule stays out of the way.",
  "tests": "SidebarRevealOnHoverSubscriberTest (the CSS) and spec sidebar-reveal-on-hover.spec.js (strip, hover, keyboard focus, click on an item, no slide on page load, light and dark, below 768px)."
```

## Traps

- A style that moves the layout (a margin of #app-content) must go in the <head>, never on page.header.left: that hook is inside #app-content, painted before its <style> arrives, and core animates the margin of #app-content for 0.3s, so every page load slid from 230px to 15px (and any spec that measured right after a load got a moving target). Spec: "a page load starts at 15px".
- A spec that clicks a left-menu item first moves the mouse over the strip (`page.mouse.move(7, 500)`): the closed menu is behind the strip, which takes the click.
- The ::after of the strip lies over the menu items: in the closed state an item is in the viewport for Playwright but hidden. A spec checks the right edge of the item (<= 15px), not toBeInViewport.
