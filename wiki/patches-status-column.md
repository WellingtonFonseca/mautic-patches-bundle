# Patches: the Status column and the status badge of the lists

The Status column (icon badge, second column) of the segment, campaign, user, email and custom field lists, the reentry badge of the campaign list and the `.patches-badge` look that is the twin of the n8n badge. What must not break: the icon follows core's toggle icon, the column is added once per drawing of the table, and a row with a label already ignores the name cell position. The row menu item Ativar / Desativar and the other list details stay in wiki/patches-lists-status.md.

Padrão: [ui-status-column.md](../../wiki/ui-status-column.md), [ui-badge.md](../../wiki/ui-badge.md).
