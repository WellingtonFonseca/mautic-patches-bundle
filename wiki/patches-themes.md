# Patches: dark and light theme fixes

Fixes of the Dark, Solarized Dark and Light themes: striped table rows, button hover, the toast, modal form buttons, the footer of the confirmation modal, white columns, table lines and the light gray page. Each entry says the cause in core. The rules that apply to every theme fix: [ui-theme.md](ui-theme.md).
