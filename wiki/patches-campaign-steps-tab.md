# Patches: the "Etapas" tab of the campaign page

An "Etapas" (Steps) tab on the campaign page that replaces core's read-only "Ações" and "Condições" tabs (both hidden by CSS). Each step, an action or a condition, is one row in the order of the flow, with a status badge (n8n dispatch steps only), a type badge, three badges (completed, failed, waiting) and its name. The failed and waiting badges are buttons: a click loads the contacts of that state of that step into ONE table under the rows. It is the ONLY place that lists the contacts of a step: the counts of a node on the Preview tab open nothing. What must not break: core's template is not touched (its tab is hidden, not removed).

## Traps

- Padrão: [ui-badge.md](../../wiki/ui-badge.md) (nowrap of an opening badge box; here `.patches-steps-badges`).
- The test status color (#7e57c2) is the same as the Ação type badge; only the icon tells them apart.
- Padrão: [ui-tooltip.md](ui-tooltip.md) (data-trigger="hover" on the badge tooltips).

  "read only": "core's _events.html.twig: per step the name, the type, a % label, the completed count (yellow) and the pending count (gray), and a bar. No button, no menu.",
  "pending": "pending = logged - processed (CampaignController::processCampaignEvents) = the scheduled logs without a failed log (contacts waiting for their time).",
